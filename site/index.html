TaxPub

TaxPub is an extension of the NISO JATS Journal Publishing DTD (v1.3, ANSI/NISO Z39.96-2021) for encoding articles containing taxonomic treatments and other taxonomy-specific features.

TaxPub v1.0

  • DTD (modular); its modules resolve relative to it
  • DTD (flattened, single file)
  • Formal Public Identifier: -//TaxPub//DTD Taxonomic Treatment Publishing DTD v1.0 20230203//EN
